MINECRAFT 2D

Chop trees. Mine rocks. Dig dirt. Build your world.

▶ Play

How to play

Axe

Removes trees

Pickaxe

Removes rocks

Shovel

Removes dirt

  1. 1 Click a tool in the toolbar. It lights up.
  2. 2 Click a block in the world.
  3. 3 Right tool for the block? It disappears!
  4. 4 The block lands in your inventory.
  5. 5 Click an inventory block to place it back.

Stuck? Press Reset World to start over.
